About the Project

Smart Food Waste Reduction System • Technology, Architecture & Global Impact

Sustainable Development

Targeting Zero Hunger & Responsible Consumption

Globally, approximately 1.3 billion tonnes of food are wasted every year—equivalent to one-third of all food produced for human consumption. In addition to ethical and economic impacts, food waste accounts for 8-10% of global greenhouse gas emissions.

The Smart Food Waste Reduction System empowers consumers, cafeteria operators, and hospitality kitchens to proactively address this crisis through inventory monitoring, intelligent shelf-life tracking, Scikit-learn machine learning forecasts, and safe surplus food donation.

System Architecture & Workflow

1. Three-Tier Expiry Classification

Every active food item is checked dynamically against today's date:

Fresh Remaining days > 3. Safe, optimal nutritional state.
Expiring Soon Remaining days 0 to 3. Actionable urgency triggers recommendations.
Expired Days < 0. Strictly excluded from consumption & donation.
2. Machine Learning Regression Pipeline

Features evaluated: Category, Quantity, Shelf Life, Days Remaining, Storage Type, Consumers, Pace.

  • Categorical variables are encoded using OneHotEncoder.
  • Continuous metrics are standardized via StandardScaler.
  • RandomForestRegressor outputs estimated waste percentage and volume.
  • Automated mitigation logic delivers category-specific advice.
3. Food Safety Guardrails in Donation

To protect vulnerable recipient populations, our system enforces strict safety checks: any food marked past its expiry date or improperly stored cannot be listed for donation. Users can easily reserve and track donation completion with recipient shelters.

4. Data Export & Auditing

Provides built-in CSV export generation for hospitality audit logs, restaurant tax incentives on charitable food donations, and academic waste research.
