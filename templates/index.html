Quick Kitchen Sustainability Tips
1. First In, First Out (FIFO): Always move older ingredients to the front of your shelves so they are used first.
2. Regulate Fridge Temperatures: Maintain the main compartment between 1°C and 4°C to double dairy shelf life.
3. Separate Ethylene Fruits: Keep apples, avocados, and bananas separate from vegetables to prevent premature ripening.
4. Freeze Surplus Bread: Slice fresh artisanal bread and freeze immediately to preserve peak taste for months.
