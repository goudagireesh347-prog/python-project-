System Recommendations & Interventions

Tailored strategies based on your waste trends and inventory dynamics

Optimize Purchase Volumes

If your top wasted category is {{ top_wasted_category }}, consider purchasing smaller quantities more frequently or switching to smaller pack sizes.

Storage Temperature Audit

Verify that your refrigerator is kept between 1°C and 4°C and freezer at -18°C. Every 2°C increase can accelerate bacterial spoilage by 40%.

Meal Prep & FIFO Method

Follow the "First-In, First-Out" protocol. When unpacking new groceries, pull existing batches to the front shelf for dinner planning.

Surplus Donation Channel

List surplus items on your Food Donation Board at least 2 days before the expiry date so local charity volunteers have sufficient pickup lead time.
